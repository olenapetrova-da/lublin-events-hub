Goal / DoD:
- Read `raw_events` (9 cols): Title | Date | Time | Venue | Category | Link | Payment for Entry | Source | _EndDate

- Rules:

  - event_id: sha1( source + '|' + normalize(title) + '|' + start_dt + '|' + normalize(venue) )
- One pass, batch read & batch write (overwrite whole `events` tab); no external calls
